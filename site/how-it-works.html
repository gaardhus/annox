How a review goes

Whoever takes part, a person in their editor or an agent at the command line, works on the same plain files. Every action writes one event to .annox/, next to the document.

1. Annotate

Highlight a passage, comment on it, or suggest replacing it. Each note is anchored by its position and the quoted text. Drafts can stay local until you publish them. Anchoring (§3)

2. Discuss

Reply in a thread beside the text. When the document changes, each note finds its quote again, or is flagged as orphaned instead of guessing. Every edit to a note stays in its history. Data model (§2)

3. Decide

Resolve a comment, or reopen it. Accept a suggestion to apply the edit, or reject or withdraw it. An accepted edit is undone with a new suggestion that reverts it, so the record stays intact. Suggestions (§4)

4. Share

annox commit commits the new event files, and you push them like code. Or point the workspace at a hub, and events and cursors arrive as collaborators type. Sync (§7)

In your editor

Here it is in Neovim: replying to a comment, commenting on a selection, accepting a suggestion, and suggesting edits as you type.
