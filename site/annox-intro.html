annox
Highlights, comments, and suggested edits for plain-text documents, in any editor.
An open standard · stored next to your files
1Annotate

Comment on any span of text.

Sam reviews a proposal in their editor and asks for a date.

2Suggest

Agents suggest edits, like people do.

Claude proposes a replacement. Nothing changes until someone accepts it.

3Store

Every action writes one event file.

Plain JSON in .annox/, next to the document. Nothing is written into the text itself.

4Anchor

Annotations follow the text as it changes.

Each one remembers its quote and context, so edits elsewhere don't knock it loose.

5Decide

Accept, and the edit is applied.

Then reply and resolve the thread. The history stays in the event log.
